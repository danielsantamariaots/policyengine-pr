Bona fide residents of Puerto Rico claim the Additional Child Tax Credit on
Form 1040-SS, Part II. Unlike Schedule 8812 filers, their refundable credit is
limited by Social Security and Medicare taxes paid (Part II, lines 12–18),
not by 15% of earned income over $2,500. Statutory basis: IRC §24(d)(1)(B)(ii)
and §24(k) (Puerto Rico), as amended; the compiled IRC text is not in this
folder.

Payroll tax rates (6.2% / 1.45% employee share) are cited to IRS Topic
no. 751 (https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc751, read 2026-11-03); the 2025
wage base ($176,100) to the Form 1040-SS instructions.
