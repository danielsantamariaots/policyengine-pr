BI RI 26-01 (17 Feb 2026) is the Secretary's notice of the 2025 inflation
adjustment under Sección 1052.01(i); its thresholds and maxima match the
Anejo CT form line by line.

Note: the 2025 return has no Puerto Rico child tax credit. Line 27D
("Créditos reembolsables del Gobierno Federal") carries the federal
Additional Child Tax Credit claimed on IRS Form 1040-SS.
