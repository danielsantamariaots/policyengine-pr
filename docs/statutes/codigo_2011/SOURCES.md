`codigo.txt` is the pdftotext extraction. Key passages (page numbers are the
compilation's own):

- Sección 1021.01(a)(3), p. 29-30: rate table for taxable years after 2012.
- Sección 1021.01(b)(4)-(5)(E), p. 30: gradual adjustment, 5% over $500,000,
  limit $8,895 + 33% of exemptions (years after 2014).
- Sección 1021.01(c), p. 31: 95% factor (years after 2018); 92% for gross
  income not exceeding $100,000 (years after 2019, per Ley 40-2020).
- Sección 1031.02(a)(36), p. 110: youth exemption, first $40,000, ages 16-26
  at year end (years after 2013). The 2025 Hacienda forms cite this as
  paragraph (37), so it was renumbered by an amendment after January 2022.

Amendments after 16 January 2022 are not in this compilation; the 2025
Hacienda instructions remain the check for current amounts.
