## Rules (Reglamento 7653)

- Categories: A elderly, B blind, C needy families with children (the
  federal TANF group), D totally and permanently disabled adults, G
  general assistance, T children with guardians.
- **Category C eligibility** (Sec. 4.16): preliminary test — gross earned
  and unearned income of the unit must not exceed the "total de
  necesidades" inflated by 185%; otherwise the unit is ineligible.
- **Deductions** (Secs. 4.18–4.20): production costs for self-employment
  (fixed $75 in A, B, D, G, T; $90 in C); in Category C a $90 deduction
  per earner from wages, salary or self-employment income, applied per
  member; in A and D the first $20 plus half of the next $60; in B the
  first $85 plus half of the remainder.
- **Earned-income exemptions for new jobs** (Sec. 4.20): 100% of net
  earnings months 1–3, 75% months 4–9, within a 60-month limit; child care
  deduction up to $175/month per child over 2 (full-time) or $200 under 2,
  $87/$100 part-time.
- **Benefit** (Sec. 4.22–4.23): "necesidades básicas ajustadas" less net
  income; checks under $10 (C) or $5 (other categories) not issued; cents
  dropped. If net income exceeds adjusted basic needs the unit is
  ineligible.
- **Time limit** (Sec. 4.24): 60 months in Category C.
- Two-parent families without a disabled parent are not served (WRD).

## Amounts (official, 2023)

- Family of three, no income: maximum benefit **$584/month** (WRD Figure 5).
- Family of three: initial-eligibility earnings ceiling **$1,620/month**
  (WRD Figure 2); 1,620 / 1.85 = $875.68, so the standard of need for a
  family of three is about $876 if the WRD figure is the gross test alone.
- Categories A and D: **$100/month** since September 2023.
