# Medicaid.gov — Medicaid & CHIP in Puerto Rico (read 2026-11-04)

Source: https://www.medicaid.gov/state-overviews/puerto-rico.html (the page is
rendered by JavaScript; the saved .html does not contain the table, so it is
transcribed here from the rendered page).

> **Medicaid and CHIP Standard Monthly Income Eligibility Levels effective
> April 1, 2026 to April 2027**
>
> Eligibility in Puerto Rico differs from eligibility in the states. Puerto
> Rico uses the federal poverty level (FPL), to determine eligibility. Most
> Medicaid eligibility and CHIP eligibility is based on Modified adjusted
> gross income (MAGI). The following table provides a more detailed view of
> Puerto Rico's MAGI-based eligibility levels, expressed as a percentage of
> the Level (PRPL) for Medicaid and CHIP eligibility groups:
>
> | Household Size | Medicaid | CHIP |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | $1,835.00 | $3,604.00 |
> | 2 | $2,489.00 | $4,887.00 |
> | 3 | $3,142.00 | $6,170.00 |
> | 4 | $3,795.00 | $7,453.00 |
>
> As of January 2026, Puerto Rico enrolled 1.3 million individuals in
> Medicaid and CHIP. Source: https://medicaid.pr.gov/CMS/20

Observations (ours, not the page's):

- The Medicaid column equals 138% of the 2026 HHS poverty guideline for the
  48 states ($15,960 for one person; $5,680 per additional person): 1.38 ×
  15,960 / 12 = $1,835.40; the increment $653–654 = 1.38 × 5,680 / 12. The
  CHIP column equals 271% of the same guideline (266% plus the 5-percentage
  point MAGI disregard): 2.71 × 15,960 / 12 = $3,604.30; increment 2.71 ×
  5,680 / 12 = $1,282.73. The table therefore extends to larger households
  by adding $653.44 (Medicaid) and $1,282.73 (CHIP) per member.
- The page does not give the April 2025 – March 2026 levels. Applying the
  same percentages to the 2025 guideline ($15,650; $5,500 per person) gives
  $1,799.75 (+$632.50) for Medicaid and $3,534.29 (+$1,242.08) for CHIP;
  these are derived, not published, and are flagged as such in the
  parameter file.
